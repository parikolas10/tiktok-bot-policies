# Privacy Policy for TikTok AI Influencer Bot

**Last Updated: August 2026**

## 1. Information We Collect
Our TikTok AI Influencer Bot ("the Bot") collects the following information:
- TikTok username and profile information (via Login Kit)
- Video content generated by the AI system
- Basic analytics (views, likes, comments) via TikTok Webhooks

## 2. How We Use Information
We use the collected information to:
- Generate and post AI-created videos to your TikTok account
- Optimize content based on performance metrics
- Improve the AI algorithms for better engagement

## 3. Data Storage
- All user data is stored locally on your device
- We do not store any data on external servers
- TikTok API tokens are stored only in your local `.env` file

## 4. Third-Party Services
We use the following third-party services:
- **TikTok API**: For authentication and content posting
- **Groq API**: For AI script generation
- **ElevenLabs API**: For text-to-speech voiceover
- **Pexels & Pixabay**: For stock video footage

## 5. Your Rights
You can:
- Revoke access at any time via your TikTok account settings
- Delete all generated videos from your device
- Request removal of any content posted by the Bot
